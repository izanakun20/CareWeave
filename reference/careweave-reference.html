Findings that shape the product

  • Triage level and department explain the predictable part of a wait. A richer model did not beat the simple one.
  • Hour, weekday, month, age, sex, insurance, visit type and chronic condition showed no usable signal. Average wait by hour stays between 50 and 54 minutes.
  • About 17 minutes of random spread remains per visit, so QueueSight shows ranges, not single promises.
  • Weekly alert limits at 3 standard deviations fired 2 times in 624 department-weeks, close to chance. The real problem is structural, not a sudden spike.
